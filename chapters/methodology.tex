This chapter outlines the mathematical foundations and methodologies 
underlying the investigation of memorization in deep neural networks. The methodology is structured systematically:
beginning with the mathematical background of neural networks and training, formalizing the concept of memorization, an exploration of white-box attack techniques, and an examination of explainable AI (XAI) methods to reveal and interpret memorized representations in image classification models. Finally, we detail the experimental setup, audit pipeline, and evaluation metrics used to analyze the results.
